Pyrotechnic designers, Valencia. We write fireworks to music the way a composer writes for an orchestra: every shell has a part, a colour and a beat.

The films on this page were not filmed. They were rendered by the same engine that plays the composer below.

The colour is a metal.

A star burns the colour of the salt packed into it. Strontium gives the red, sodium the gold, barium the green, copper the blue that is hardest to make, and magnesium the white heat behind all of them.

Single shells

Write a show on sixteen beats.

Each row is a shell, each column a beat. A shell climbs for over a second before it breaks, so the composer fires every rocket early: it bursts on the beat, not after it.
