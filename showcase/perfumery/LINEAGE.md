# Lineage — Maison Orvé (`showcase/perfumery`)

A showcase model: design first, performance last. It is a worked example of a blend (`skill/references/23-lineage-and-blend.md`), not a template to copy.

| Role | Lineage | Principle | Axis | Becomes, here | Not copied |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dominant 55% | Emil Ruder, *Typographie* (1967) | White space is a form, equal to the printed form | Density, composition | One narrow column of type against a wide field of water; the garden is a ruled table | His specimen layouts |
| Counter 30% | April Greiman, *Design Quarterly* #133 (1986) | Hybrid digital imagery in layers | Imagery | A particle field drawn under the type in the colours of the chosen notes | Her collage itself |
| Accent 15% | Bruno Munari, *Design as Art* (1966) | Reading made playful | Interaction | Composing the accord is play: three choices, and the water and the formula answer | His books |

- **Tension:** Ruder's still white field against Greiman's moving, layered image.
- **Subject-only ingredient:** the perfume pyramid itself: top notes rise and fade first, the base sinks and stays.
- **Signature move:** ink in water, released at the depth of each tier and stirred by the visitor's finger.
- **Dials:** ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 3. Quiet type, a living image.
- **Technological proof:** a curl-noise particle field redrawn every frame (no 8-bit fade drift), per-particle shear and diffusion, a pre-warmed first frame and a still under reduced motion.

## Mobile composition

The water becomes a sticky band at the top of the screen while you compose under it; the garden table drops its middle column.

## Priorities

Design leads every decision here. The page spends GPU time, large type and live rendering freely; it still keeps the floor: real contrast, a keyboard path, reduced motion designed as a still, all copy readable without JavaScript, and nothing that pretends to send.
