100metres across the frame

The dark is kept here.

Cerro Lumen sits at 2,840 m, fifty kilometres from the nearest streetlight. On moonless nights we open the dome to twelve visitors and point a 4.1 m mirror wherever they ask.

The dome

Thirty-one metres of aluminium that turns in four minutes. Inside, the floor is kept at the night's own temperature so the air above the mirror stays still.
