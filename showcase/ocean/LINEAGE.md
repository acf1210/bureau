# Lineage — Maré (`showcase/ocean`)

A showcase model: design first, performance last. It is a worked example of a blend (`skill/references/23-lineage-and-blend.md`), not a template to copy.

| Role | Lineage | Principle | Axis | Becomes, here | Not copied |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dominant 55% | Ladislav Sutnar, *Catalog Design Progress* (1950) | Information is structure; signals tell readers where they are | Content logic | One gauge reads the depth all the way down; every zone is named by its depth | His catalogue spreads |
| Counter 30% | Lance Wyman, Mexico 68 Olympics identity | Lines radiate from the name until it becomes pattern | Identity | Iso-lines of a distance field around the wordmark drift outward like swell | The 68 logotype |
| Accent 15% | Susan Kare, original Macintosh icons (1984) | Meaning inside a tiny grid | Icons | The Beaufort scale drawn as thirteen pictograms on a 12-unit grid | The Mac icons |

- **Tension:** Sutnar's sober signals against Wyman's radiating pattern.
- **Subject-only ingredient:** a fully developed sea answers its wind in a known way (Pierson–Moskowitz), and the harbour breathes on a 12 h 25 min cycle.
- **Signature move:** the name's echoes become the sea; below, a ray-marched ocean builds with the wind.
- **Dials:** ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 3. Loud name, loud sea, calm descent.
- **Technological proof:** a chamfer distance transform with marching squares for the contours; a ray-marched height field with Fresnel sky and wind-driven foam; a single-harmonic tide.

## Mobile composition

The gauge becomes a bar across the top during the descent; the sea is a window above its controls; cruise legs stack their descriptions.
