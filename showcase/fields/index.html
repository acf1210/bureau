786 km

Two satellites, five days

Sentinel-2 is a pair of satellites on one orbit, phased 180° apart. Each comes back over the same field every 10 days; together, every 5 at the equator, more often at higher latitudes.

13: four at 10 m, six at 20 m, three at 60 m

B8 at 842 nm, 10 m

Clouds are the first thing a pass has to see through, and an optical sensor cannot.

14 km

A mile, then a quarter

The land was surveyed before it was farmed. A section is a mile square, 640 acres; a quarter section is 160. A centre pivot sprinkler swings a circle inside a quarter, or across a whole section, from a single well.

In Finney County, Kansas, the circles are 800 and 1,600 m across, watered partly from the Ogallala Aquifer.

The survey lines draw first; the ground fills in after them.

5 km, 10 m pixels, B8 and B4

Vigour

Chlorophyll absorbs red light; leaf structure scatters near infrared. The difference, normalised, is the vegetation index:

NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)

At 10 m an 800 m pivot is about 5,000 pixels: enough to see a dry arc, not a single plant.

Crop

The season’s curve gives each field away. USDA’s Cropland Data Layer classifies every field in the contiguous United States each year, at 30 m from 2008 to 2023 and at 10 m from 2024.

The classes here are ours: six colours, labelled where they grow.

  • corn
  • winter wheat
  • sorghum
  • alfalfa
  • soybeans
  • grass, fallow

1.1 km, 20 m pixels, B4, B5, B7

Stress

Crop disease changes how a canopy reflects the red edge. The red-edge bands B5 and B7 are native 20 m, so the pixel coarsens here. A Sentinel-2 index built on bands 4, 5 and 7 identified wheat yellow rust with 84.1% overall accuracy against field surveys.

Further out, satellites read the conditions pests need: soil moisture with vegetation flagged favourable swarming ground about 70 days before the November 2016 desert locust outbreak in Mauritania.

An alert is a place to send someone with boots, not a diagnosis.

70 m, 0.5 m pixels

Herd

At 10 m a steer covers under 2% of one pixel. At 50 cm, the resolution Pléiades delivers, it is four pixels long, and a model can count it. Published detectors already count cattle and elk on pasture this way.
