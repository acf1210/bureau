Feature 01

The intersection has a heartbeat.

At 02:14, the city stops being a diagram and becomes a room. We spent three nights with the people who keep it lit.

The first thing you notice is not the noise. It is the choreography: a bus that waits four extra seconds, a cyclist who does not, a traffic controller who lifts one finger and changes the mood of a square. Automation is a promise of consistency. The night is a negotiation.

We met Ana at the edge of the Baixa at 02:00. She has operated the same intersection for eleven years. Her console is a metal box with eight levers and a fan that sounds like it is thinking. She does not use the word “flow”. She says: “I let people finish their thought.”

“A city is not a machine that runs. It is a conversation that continues.”

By 04:00 the square has changed hands three times. The delivery vans leave; the cleaners arrive; the first runners appear like a rumour. The controller stays. The lights keep their rhythm. Nothing about it is automatic.
