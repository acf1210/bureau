Halflight

A light study in three movements. The room, the body, the eye: each one ten times closer than the last.

18–21 Nov 2026 · LX Factory, LisbonLight: ember

Enter the program

Three scales, one score.

Seventy-two minutes, no interval. Each movement moves the frame ten times closer, from the hall to the back of your eye.

  1. I, 19:3010 mHall A

    Ember

    A low, warm field fills the hall. The first movement is the last ten minutes of daylight, when a room starts to hold colour instead of reflecting it.

  2. II, 20:251 mHall A

    Cobalt

    The field cools. A single line of blue crosses the floor at walking pace, at the height of your hands. Follow it, or stand still and let it pass through you.

  3. III, 21:051 cmHall B

    Afterimage

    The room goes dark for ninety seconds. What remains is the image your eye makes without light, the only part of the work you take home.

Light is not a surface.

It is a duration you walk through.

The work renders in real time. No two visits produce the same frame: the shader is the score, the room is the instrument, and the visitor sets the tempo.

Four nights. Eighty people a night.

Entry is timed. The last movement is seated and silent. If you need a low-sensory session, write to us: we hold one hour each afternoon.
