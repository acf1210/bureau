Three movements, one horizon.

Each night runs the same score at a different speed. The room is never the same twice.

Score

Duration 72 minutes, no interval. The installation responds to the number of people in the room; silence is part of the instrument.

19:30 I. Ember Hall A

A low, warm field. The first movement is about the last ten minutes of daylight — the moment a room begins to hold colour instead of reflecting it.

20:25 II. Cobalt Hall A

The field shifts to a colder register. A single line of blue moves across the floor at walking pace; visitors are invited to follow it or stand still.

21:05 III. Afterimage Hall B

The room goes dark for ninety seconds. What remains is the image your eyes make without light — the only part of the work you take home.

Documentation · 2025

What the room remembers.

Filmed during the first study in Porto. The final work is site-specific: the room at LX Factory has a different ceiling, a different echo, a different dark.

Light is not a surface. It is a duration — something you move through, not something you look at.

Studio note The work is built in real time. No two visits produce the same frame. The shader is the score; the room is the instrument; the visitor is the tempo.

Visit

Four nights. One room.

Entry is timed. The installation holds 80 people; the last movement is silent and seated. If you need a low-sensory session, write to us — we hold one hour each afternoon.
