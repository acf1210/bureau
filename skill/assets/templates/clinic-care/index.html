Four kinds of visit.

Each one has a room of its own and a person who will explain the result to you before you leave.

  • Lung function test

    Spirometry and gas transfer: you breathe into a mouthpiece, hard and then slowly.

    Takes
    40 min
    Bring
    your inhalers

  • Sleep study

    One night in a quiet room, with sensors that record breathing, oxygen and movement.

    Takes
    one night
    Bring
    your pillow

  • Asthma and COPD care

    A plan for daily treatment and for bad days, reviewed with the same doctor every visit.

    Takes
    30 min
    Bring
    a symptom diary

  • Breathing rehabilitation

    Eight weekly sessions of exercise and breathing technique, in small groups.

    Takes
    8 weeks
    Bring
    trainers

What happens on the day.

No surprises. If anything in this list worries you, call us before you come and we will talk it through.

  1. Arrive ten minutes early.The waiting room is on the ground floor, with step-free access from Calle Gorriti.
  2. Skip your inhaler for four hours.Only for a lung function test, and only if your doctor agrees.
  3. The test itself.A respiratory technician stays with you the whole time and tells you what each step is for.
  4. Leave with the result explained.A written summary in plain Spanish or English, and a copy sent to your doctor if you wish.
