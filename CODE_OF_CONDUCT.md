# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want BUREAU to be a place where people can do precise, ambitious work without harassment, gatekeeping, or performative cruelty.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the work, not the person.
- Be specific. "This is bad" is not feedback; "the accent appears seven times above the fold" is.
- Credit references and collaborators.
- Respect licenses, privacy, and consent.
- Make room for beginners without lowering the standard.
- Disagree in public, resolve in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized attention or imagery.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Plagiarism or unlicensed assets.
- Deliberate fabrication presented as fact.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer via the contact in `package.json`. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.
